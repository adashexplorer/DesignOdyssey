# FR vs NFR
- FR (Functional Requirements)
- NFR (Non-Functional Requirements)

> FR represents **WHAT** of your system while NFR represents **HOW** of your system.<br>
> In other way, FR emphasizes `What functionalties or behaviour your system should have ?`
> NFR emphasizes `How your system should behave ?`

## Why these are important ?
- Developers need this to build a clean & effective system.
- Stakeholders need this to ensure their business requirements / objectives meet.
- QAs need this to benchmarking their test cases to validate both **WHAT** & **HOW**

## Non-Functional Requirements :
### Criteria -
- Performance
- Scalability
- Reliability
- Usability
- Security
- Maintainability
